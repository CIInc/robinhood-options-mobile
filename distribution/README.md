## Asset Specifications

### Google Play Console
- **App Icon (`store-listing/app-icon.png`)**: 512 × 512 PNG, 32-bit color RGBA.
- **Feature Graphic (`store-listing/feature-graphic.png`)**: 1024 × 500 PNG.
- **Phone Screenshots (`store-listing/phone-*.png`)**: 1080 × 1920 PNG (9:16 aspect ratio).
- **Release Notes (`whatsnew/whatsnew-en-US`)**: Plain text, max 500 characters.

### Apple App Store Connect
- **Screenshots (`app-store/screenshots/01-*.png`)**: 1206 × 2622 PNG (6.7" / 6.9" Super Retina XDR).
